Being dependable doesn’t mean saying yes before checking your week.

  • See your own work and your team’s needs in one place
  • Choose a few outcomes with clear reasons and realistic time
  • Protect focus while keeping a route open for urgent support
  • Explain the gap and offer a choice someone can act on

A plan cannot make impossible demand disappear. It can help you make the decisions visible and agree changes earlier.

BEFORE YOU BEGIN

A few useful
details.

What does the workbook add to a template?

You work through explanations and a realistic example, make decisions for your own situation, rehearse the conversation and check your approach against the guidance. The reusable worksheet brings those decisions together.

Who is this workbook for?

New team leaders balancing their own deliverables with meetings, team questions and incoming requests. Use your actual week or the fictional examples if you prefer.

How is this different from a weekly planner?

You will practise the decisions behind the plan: estimate capacity, choose priorities, negotiate a change and check the impact on other people. The planner is the final reusable output.

What if I cannot change the workload myself?

The exercises help you distinguish what you can decide from what needs agreement. You will prepare a capacity estimate, options and a specific question for the person who can change scope, timing, ownership or resources. The workbook cannot guarantee their agreement.

Is it a video course or live session?

No. It is a self-guided workbook: two printable PDFs you keep, with no videos or live sessions. Allow about 60 minutes on your own. The companion guide also includes an optional 90-minute plan if you want to practise with a group or a partner.

Do I need another workbook first?

No. This workbook includes its own workload discussion structure and practice cases. Prepare Your Next Difficult Conversation is a separate option for deeper preparation for everyday conversations.

Can I type into the workbook?

These are printable PDFs with writing space, not interactive form fields. Print them, use your PDF annotation tool, or write your answers separately. No workplace information needs to be uploaded.
