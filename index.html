A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

A clear idea
before you start.

Are these for new team leaders?

Yes. They focus on everyday challenges of leading a team: having clearer conversations while balancing your own work and other people’s needs and sharing ownership of tasks. You can use your real situation or the fictional practice cases.

What do I receive?

Each workshop contains a printable visual workbook and a companion teaching guide. Work independently in about 60 minutes, or use the guide for a 90-minute facilitated session. These offers do not include video lessons or a booked live session.

Can I see the exercises first?

Yes. Each workshop page includes a preview. Get 3 free pages by email.

Why choose a workshop when free advice is available?

Free advice can be enough when you need an idea. These workshops give you a sequence to work through: a realistic example, space to apply it, a rehearsal, answer guidance and a reusable plan. Preview the actual exercises to decide whether that structure would help you.

Can I work through it without printing?

Yes. Read on screen and use a PDF annotation tool or separate notes. The worksheets have writing space but are not interactive forms. Work on your own, or practise with a partner. Video lessons and a facilitator are not included.

Do I need to take them in order?

No. All three are complete, independent workshops. Choose the need that matters most right now. Each contains the exercises and scripts needed for its topic, so another purchase is not required.

Can I buy a workshop now?
