A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

A clear idea
before you start.

Are these for new team leaders?

Yes. They focus on everyday challenges of leading a team: having clearer conversations while balancing your own work and other people’s needs and sharing ownership of tasks. You can use your real situation or the fictional practice cases.

What do I receive?

Each one comes as two printable PDFs: a visual workbook you fill in and a companion guide that explains each step. Work through it on your own in about 60 minutes. There are no video lessons or live sessions: you get the material to keep and reuse. The guide also includes an optional 90-minute plan if you want to use it with a group.

Can I see the exercises first?

Yes. Each workbook page includes a preview. Get 3 free samples by email.

Preview the workweek workbook ↗︎Preview the delegation workbook ↗︎Preview the conversation workbook ↗︎
Why choose a workbook when free advice is available?

Free advice can be enough when you need an idea. These workbooks give you a sequence to work through: a realistic example, space to apply it, a rehearsal, answer guidance and a reusable plan. Preview the actual exercises to decide whether that structure would help you.

Can I work through it without printing?

Yes. Read on screen and use a PDF annotation tool or separate notes. The worksheets have writing space but are not interactive forms. Work on your own, or practise with a partner. Video lessons and a facilitator are not included.

Do I need to take them in order?

No. All three are complete, independent workbooks. Choose the need that matters most right now. Each contains the exercises and scripts needed for its topic, so another purchase is not required.

Can I buy a workbook now?
