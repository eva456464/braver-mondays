THE FEELING YOU’RE WORKING TOWARD

“I know what I need
to say. And I’m ready
to listen.”

You don’t need a perfect script. You need a clear starting point and something to return to when the conversation gets difficult.

  • Say what matters without guessing at someone’s motives
  • Listen with two questions you’ve thought through
  • Have a response ready for disagreement or defensiveness
  • Work toward clear actions, support and a follow-up date

Preparation can’t control someone else’s reaction. It gives you something steadier to bring to the conversation.

A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Before you
begin.

What does the workshop add to a template?

You work through explanations and a realistic example, make decisions for your own situation, rehearse the conversation and check your approach against the guidance. The reusable worksheet brings those decisions together.

Is this right for a first-time team leader?

Yes. It is designed for people in their first two years of team leadership, including people managing former peers. Choose an everyday situation such as a missed commitment, interruptions or competing priorities.

Is this a video course or a live session?

It is a self-guided workshop delivered through printable PDF materials. There are no video lessons or booked live sessions in this offer. The companion guide provides a plan for a 90-minute facilitated practice session.

What if I have a conversation coming up soon?

The full independent workshop takes about 60 minutes, including spoken practice. After working through it, use the 30-minute return route and the one-page planner for your next conversation. Give yourself more time for complex situations.

Can I use it without printing?

Yes. Read on screen and use your PDF reader’s annotation tools or separate notes. The worksheets are printable PDFs, not interactive forms. No account or video lessons are needed to work through the materials.

Do I need to share a personal situation?

No. Use a fictional case or keep your own notes private. You can practise aloud on your own or with a partner. You do not need to upload employee information or a completed workbook.

Will it make every conversation go smoothly?

No workshop can guarantee another person’s response. This one helps you prepare a fair account, listen to a different view and make the next step clearer. Those are things you can practise.

What kinds of situations need another route?

Formal employment action, harassment or discrimination complaints, safeguarding concerns and safety risks need the appropriate organisational process and qualified support. This workshop focuses on everyday communication.
