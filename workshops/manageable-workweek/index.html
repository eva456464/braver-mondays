BEFORE YOU BEGIN

A few useful
details.

What does the workshop add to a template?

You work through explanations and a realistic example, make decisions for your own situation, rehearse the conversation and check your approach against the guidance. The reusable worksheet brings those decisions together.

Who is this workshop for?

New team leaders balancing their own deliverables with meetings, team questions and incoming requests. Use your actual week or the fictional examples if you prefer.

How is this different from a weekly planner?

You will practise the decisions behind the plan: estimate capacity, choose priorities, negotiate a change and check the impact on other people. The planner is the final reusable output.

What if I cannot change the workload myself?

The exercises help you distinguish what you can decide from what needs agreement. You will prepare a capacity estimate, options and a specific question for the person who can change scope, timing, ownership or resources. The workshop cannot guarantee their agreement.

Is it a video course or live session?

This offer contains two printable PDFs, with no videos or booked live sessions. Allow about 60 minutes independently. The companion guide also supports a 90-minute facilitated session and partner practice.

Do I need another workshop first?

No. This workshop includes its own workload discussion structure and practice cases. Prepare Your Next Difficult Conversation is a separate option for deeper preparation for everyday conversations.

Can I type into the workbook?

These are printable PDFs with writing space, not interactive form fields. Print them, use your PDF annotation tool, or write your answers separately. No workplace information needs to be uploaded.
