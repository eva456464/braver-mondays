BEFORE YOU BEGIN

A few useful
details.

What does the workshop add to a template?

You work through explanations and a realistic example, make decisions for your own situation, rehearse the conversation and check your approach against the guidance. The reusable worksheet brings those decisions together.

Is this for a first-time team leader?

Yes. It is designed for everyday tasks you are authorised to delegate, especially when you are used to doing the work yourself. Start with a small, bounded task or use the fictional case.

Does delegation mean leaving someone to figure it out?

No. You will agree the result, decision boundaries, resources, learning support and checkpoints. You still own the responsibilities and approvals your role requires.

What if the team is already at capacity?

The workshop starts with that check. You will identify what would need to change in scope, timing or existing commitments before adding the work. Delegation should not simply move overload to someone else.

Should I never take a task back?

Sometimes intervention or reassignment is necessary. The exercises help you distinguish a preference from a quality gap or material risk, then make any change to ownership explicit.

Is it a live session or video course?

This offer contains a workbook and a teaching guide as printable PDFs. Work independently in about 60 minutes or use the 90-minute facilitated session plan. No video lessons or booked live session are included.

Do I need either of the other workshops first?

No. This workshop stands on its own, including a capacity check and a hand-off script. The conversation and workweek workshops explore their own topics in more depth.

How do I complete the exercises?

Print the pages, use your PDF annotation tool or write separately. These are writing spaces rather than interactive form fields. Use fictional names and keep your completed notes private.
